## Mesačný výkaz preddavkov: Dôvera and Union

Neither insurer offers a blank form to download. Since 1 January 2021 Dôvera accepts the monthly report only electronically, through its Elektronická pobočka (https://www.dovera.sk/platitel/zamestnavatel/tema-poistne-mesacne-vykazy/vykazy-tlaciva). Union likewise uses its own electronic portal for payers (https://www.union.sk/platitelia-zdravotneho-poistenia/). The report's form and structure are common to all insurers and are set by the Ministry of Health, so the VšZP structure above applies to them too.
